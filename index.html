How to use one topic

  1. Read the notes once. They hold the results GATE expects you to recall, not the derivations. Derive each one from your textbook at least once.
  2. Play with the simulation before solving anything. Predict what will happen when you move a slider, then move it. A wrong prediction is the most useful thing on the page.
  3. Read the real-life link. Application-style questions are easier when the formula is attached to a picture.
  4. Read the traps. Most lost marks come from sign conventions, units, and a formula used outside its conditions.
  5. Solve the practice questions on paper, then open the solution. Tick “Done” only when you got them right unaided.

A study plan around 12-hour workdays

From November 2026 to an exam in February 2028 there are about 15 months. With long shifts, steady short sessions beat occasional long ones. One topic from this site fits in a 45 to 60 minute sitting.
